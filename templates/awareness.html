Comprehensive Threat Profiles

1. "Digital Arrest" & Police Impersonation Scams

Fraudsters pose as CBI, Customs, or Police officers on Skype/WhatsApp video calls claiming your Aadhaar or FedEx parcel was caught with narcotics, demanding money for "clearance". Fact: Law enforcement never conducts video call arrests or demands money transfers.

2. SMS Phishing for PAN / Electricity Bill

Messages claiming your electricity power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM unless a bill is paid via an APK link. Fact: Electricity boards never send mobile numbers or APK download links.

3. Crypto Trading & Fake Stock Tips

WhatsApp groups offering insider stock market tips with fake institutional trading app screenshots showing 400% profit. Fact: All fake investment platforms fabricate profit numbers and refuse withdrawals.

4. SIM Swap Fraud

Attackers clone your mobile SIM card through telecom operator impersonation to intercept banking OTPs. Fact: If your mobile network suddenly disappears without reason, contact your mobile operator immediately.

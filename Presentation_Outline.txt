## Slide 3: Total Application Workflow (Step-by-Step)
**Title**: How CyberCrimeAI Works
**Content**:
1. **Initiation**: User logs in and navigates to the Dashboard.
2. **Category Selection**: Choose from 15 specialized cybercrime categories using the grid layout and live-search.
3. **AI Incident Analysis**: The AI Chat Sandbox analyzes the incident in real-time, determines the risk level, and suggests actionable checklists.
4. **Dynamic Data Collection**: AI generates dynamic questions tailored specifically to the type of crime.
5. **Review & Submit**: User previews the complaint details, uploads evidence, and submits.
6. **Report Generation**: The system generates a formal PDF report and provides a tracking reference number (e.g., CC-YYYY-NNNNN).
